Move & defendPut your thumb anywhere on the left side: the stick jumps under it. Hold away from your opponent to block; down + away blocks low.

Six attacksTop row punches, bottom row kicks: light, medium, heavy. Rest your thumb between two buttons to press both. 3P / 3K press all three.

HadoukenFacing right: roll down → down-forward → forward, then punch.

Play onlineHost online, then send the invite link or read out the four-letter code. Your friend taps Join with code. Same Wi-Fi or across the internet.

Keyboard & controllersArrows move. A / S / D punch. Z / X / C kick. Enter starts. Escape pauses. Standard gamepads work and rumble.

On iPhoneOpen in Safari, Share → Add to Home Screen. Sound plays even with the silent switch on. Rumble needs iOS 18 or later.

Connection details

The host’s phone runs the arcade and streams it to player 2 over WebRTC, directly between the devices. Very strict networks (some mobile carriers, office Wi-Fi) need a relay server; see README. A host reload creates a new room.
